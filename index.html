Orien Energy is developing a thermally driven adsorption heat pump that converts industrial low-grade waste heat directly into pressurized process steam with minimal electricity consumption.

Technology

At Orien Energy, we are developing a patent-pending heat pump that turns low-grade waste heat into valuable steam. By alternating the exothermic adsorption and endothermic desorption, our system uses waste heat to generate high-temperature steam on one side and provides cooling on the other, creating two value streams.
